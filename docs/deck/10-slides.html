Saved items already show intent. Thirty-day conversion still fails without a coupon.

Myntra · Growth PM · north star is Wishlist-to-Purchase in 30 days · no monetary incentive as the core lever

North star

W2P 30d = users who buy ≥1 wishlisted item within 30 days of saving it ÷ users who saved ≥1 item.

Constraint

No coupons, cashback, sale alerts, EOSS nags, or price-drop waits as the product. Paying for the buy is not a win.

The job

A wishlist is explicit interest that stalls. Discover why, then help them finish a judgement inside the list.

What the shopper is missing (user value)

A defensible choice — why this save, not the other two. A defensible price — whether the ask is fair for what it is. Fewer open loops — no YouTube detour before ordering. Lower risk of being wrong — fit and quality from other shoppers at the moment of doubt. Confidence is the mechanism; the felt benefit is closure.

What the business gets if that is right

W2P 30d rises through decision completion at full price, not margin given away. Return-rate exposure falls where fit is resolved before try-and-buy. Demand can move out of sale weeks if waiting is a default caused by a missing judgement aid — not a demand for money.

W2P 30d only moves if shoppers return, finish a judgement, then act

Metric tree from the architecture: Discovery informs ranking. An MVP may only target Resolve and Decide — never a discount.

1Revisit

Definition: open the wishlist within 30 days of a save.

Rationale: if they never return, nothing converts. Necessary. Not the bet.

2Resolve · secondary

Definition: blocking doubt (fit, quality, look) is gone.

Rationale: scrape ranked it #1; research keeps it real and not the decider. Ships with Decide.

3Decide · primary Lock

Definition: among ≥2 similar saves, buy or deliberately drop in 30 days.

Rationale: comparison is the only lever confirmed three independent ways. Removals count as success.

4Act

Definition: wishlist item → bag / checkout in 30 days.

Rationale: last step. A coupon tap is not this metric and is not a win.

Why Decide outranks Resolve in the lock

Counting respondents once per node: Decide 8/9, Resolve 7/9. The margin is one person — so Resolve still ships. The case for Decide-first is the unlock-versus-help evidence, not that share alone.

Sale-watchers (S3) stay a control, not the audience. Segment is S4-led (compare) with S2 (fit) secondary.

Price-waiting is ranked — then set aside

The scrape put sale-wait at rank 2 / 0.83 and sale-waitlist at rank 12 / 0.39, both price-flagged. They are counted so we do not pretend waiting is rare. They are excluded from the MVP because the brief forbids paying for conversion.

W2P 30d ← Revisit × Resolve × Decide → Act. Discovery ranks; the product only builds the non-monetary nodes.

The stalled shortlister cannot finish a judgement inside the wishlist.

Part 4 lock · generated from artefacts, not typed by hand · incentiveMvpAllowed = false

A wishlist captures interest but offers no way to finish a judgement — neither which of these is right for me nor is this worth what it costs. The only decision tool the app offers is a falling price, so intent parks on a sale that may never arrive in 30 days.

Price is the symptom of a missing judgement aid — not a demand for money.

Segment

Stalled Shortlister. Saves, returns, leaves without deciding. Eligibility: ≥2 saves in 30 days, ≤1 purchased, ≥2 of the same kind. Floors lowered from 3 because 6/9 hold only 1–5 saves. Sale-watchers are a control, not the audience. In-segment n = 2 — labelled.

Primary · Decide

Shortlist-to-decision rate: bought or deliberately dropped in 30 days. Removals are a success, not a leak. A metric that only rewards purchases would push pressure tactics — the same failure as a discount.

Secondary · Resolve

Uncertainty resolution: fit / quality from other shoppers at the moment of doubt. Must ship with Decide. Return fear was 38% of kept comments — resolve-side, not the lock.

Required · still unbuilt

Value confidence: “is this a fair price, and should I decide now?” using non-monetary signals only. Largest evidenced gap. Forbidden: coupons, price-drop alerts, EOSS, urgency.

Decision tree — proceed, re-scoped, no incentive

Price-dominant fired in its non-terminal form: 2/2 in-segment named a discount, price never held constant, but all four discount-seekers still research or compare. Fork also fired: Decide outranks Resolve, so Phase 5 is decision-first. Clean “proceed as specified” did not fire.

How to falsify this lock

Hold price constant (brief Q3/Q4 as written). If price still dominates in segment — stop. Do not ship a coupon. Also falsified if stallers name fit as the unlock more often than comparison, or if a fair-price explanation is ignored while discounts still convert.
